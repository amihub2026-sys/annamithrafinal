About Us

Welcome to Annamithra Agencies

Annamithra Agencies is a dependable food distribution partner serving professional kitchens with a growing portfolio of frozen foods, dairy products and food-service essentials.

We supply frozen snacks, momos, seafood, raw and marinated chicken, imported meats, cheese, pastry dough, sauces and kitchen essentials for hotels, restaurants, cafés, caterers and retailers.

Our focus is simple — dependable product availability, careful handling, responsive support and reliable supply that helps food businesses source more efficiently.
